bVoice is Voice, the clean audiobook player by Paul Woitaschek, with a few additions for bedtime listening. It installs beside Voice and keeps everything Voice does.

<b>What bVoice adds</b>

• <b>Books from your own server:</b> choose audiobooks from a home server and keep them on the phone, for listening offline. Set up a phone by scanning a QR code on the server's page.
• <b>Pillow speaker:</b> pair a Bluetooth speaker; the book starts when it connects and the speaker is disconnected after a pause. Quick taps on the speaker's back and forward buttons jump further.
• <b>NFC tags:</b> hold a tag to the phone to start a book. Tonie figures and plain NFC cards or stickers work; give each a book on the phone or on the server.
• <b>A listening log</b> of what was played and when, kept on the phone and, if you use a server, handed to it.

Each of these can be switched off in Settings. Without a server, bVoice is Voice plus the pillow speaker and NFC tags.

<b>Everything from Voice</b>

• Continue exactly where you stopped
• Sleep timer with gentle fade-out, bookmarks, playback speed, skip silence, auto-rewind
• DRM-free audiobook files: M4B, MP3, M4A, OGG, OGA, OPUS, in your own folders
• No account, no ads, no tracking

<b>Open source</b>

bVoice is free software under the GNU GPL v3, like Voice. The server it talks to is your own; what it must provide is documented with the source.
